# Candidates — Monday, Reviewed in One Line

Judge criteria, in order (comedy-editor lens, z-ai/glm-5.3-flash, pairwise):
1. One-line review of the entrant's own Monday (not a joke about something else).
2. Bigger laugh — a line a reader laughs at, not merely nods at.
3. Concrete detail of the Monday (meeting, commute, inbox) over generic tiredness.
4. Joke lands inside the line, no wasted words; turn arrives before the reader gives up.
5. A line a reader would repeat to a coworker the next morning.

Scored 0–5 per criterion, total /25.

## Winner

**#6** (22/25) — strongest on concrete detail (7 a.m. "quick question" → 40-minute call is a specific, lived Monday moment), the closing turn ("updating itself out of spite") lands the laugh, and the whole line is repeatable at a desk the next morning. #5 was close (21/25) but its middle ("coffee was cold, Wi-Fi died") leans on generic Monday complaints; #6's two beats are both specific.

Chosen line (exact):

Monday opened with a 7 a.m. 'quick question' that became a 40-minute call, and closed with my laptop updating itself out of spite.
